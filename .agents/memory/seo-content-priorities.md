---
name: SEO content priorities
description: The user's desired search coverage beyond jackpot terms.
---

Prioritize organic search opportunities for individual match queries (such as Arsenal vs Chelsea) and timely coach or player news (such as Mikel Arteta coverage), alongside jackpot searches.

**Why:** The user expects match and news searches to bring substantial traffic, not only jackpot searches.

**How to apply:** When planning SEO work, evaluate distinct match-preview pages and timely, useful coach/player coverage; validate demand and landing-page performance with Search Console rather than assuming jackpot traffic is the only priority.
